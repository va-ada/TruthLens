Source: U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention - Antibiotic Use: About

Published: 2025-09-23
Retrieved: 2026-10-05
Type: Government official public health agency

Antibiotics only treat certain infections caused by bacteria, such as strep throat, whooping cough and urinary tract infection (UTI).

Antibiotics do not work on viruses, such as colds and runny noses, most sore throats, flu, and most cases of chest colds. Antibiotics cannot cure viral infections like the flu or the common cold.

Antibiotics will not make you feel better if you have a virus. Taking antibiotics when you do not need them will not help you, and their side effects can still cause harm.

COVID-19 vaccines do not change or interact with your DNA in any way.

The genetic material delivered by mRNA vaccines never enters the nucleus of your cells, which is where your DNA is kept, so the vaccine does not alter your DNA. mRNA COVID-19 vaccines do not alter human DNA.
