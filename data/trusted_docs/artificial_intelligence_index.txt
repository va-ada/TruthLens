Industry produced over 90% of notable frontier AI models in 2025, and several of those models now meet or exceed human baselines on PhD-level science questions.

U.S. and Chinese AI models have traded the lead multiple times since early 2025, and the performance gap between them has narrowed.

The United States hosts 5,427 data centers, more than 10 times any other country.

U.S. private AI investment reached 285.9 billion US dollars in 2025, more than 23 times the 12.4 billion US dollars invested in China.

Generative AI reached 53% population adoption within three years, faster than the personal computer or the internet.

Documented AI incidents rose to 362, up from 233 in 2024.
